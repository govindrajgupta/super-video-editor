# Netflix vs Blockbuster reel: storyboard

Vertical documentary reel, 1080×1920 at 30fps, about 30 seconds. One voiceover line per scene;
each scene's length is cut to its line. Everything runs through the shared film look and the
12fps stop-motion step.

## Facts checked

- 2000: Netflix's founders offered to sell the company to Blockbuster for $50 million; Blockbuster declined.
- Netflix's rental model had no late fees and no physical stores.
- Blockbuster filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy in September 2010 while closing stores across the US.
- Netflix's 2025 revenue was about $45 billion.

## Characters

The people are generic, fictional stand-ins (a founder, a rental-chain boss), not likenesses of
real executives.
